# TOEFL Interview Speaking Practice Bank — Teacher Edition

## A Short Note for My Speaking Teacher

This is a practice bank for the updated TOEFL iBT **Take an Interview** speaking task. Please lead me through the questions as a real interview and help me make my answers clearer, more natural, and better developed. These are recalled practice topics, not official ETS test items.

**Practice method:** choose one topic and ask its four questions one by one. I should answer **immediately, with no preparation time**, and speak for about **45 seconds**. Please give brief feedback after each answer, then let me try again when necessary. Show the reference answer only after I have answered; it is a model for discussion, not a script to memorize.

## Main Feedback Points

Please focus on four things:

- **Content:** Did I answer every part and support my answer with specific details or examples?
- **Fluency:** Did I maintain a natural pace without too many pauses, repetitions, or filler words?
- **Pronunciation:** Was I easy to understand, with clear stress, rhythm, and intonation?
- **Language:** Were my grammar and vocabulary accurate, varied, and natural?

## Useful Answer Tips

- Start with a direct answer. Do not spend too long introducing the topic.
- Develop **two clear points**, each supported by a reason, example, or concrete detail.
- For an experience question: **context → key details → feeling, result, or lesson**.
- For a preference or opinion question: **clear position → reason and example → second reason or contrast → short conclusion**.
- Use simple transitions naturally, such as *first of all, besides, for example, however,* and *overall*.
- Avoid vague one-sentence answers and memorized-sounding templates. Personal, specific details are better.
- Finish the main idea within 45 seconds instead of adding a new point at the end.

A short comment on my strongest point and the single most important thing to improve is enough.
